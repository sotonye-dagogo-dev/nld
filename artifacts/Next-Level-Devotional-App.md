## Name

Next Level Devotional App

## Purpose

The app will host the devotional. The devotional will also be paid for in the app.

## Feature List

1. The app will host the devotional.
2. The devotional will also be paid for in the app.
3. The app will give church members the opportunity to ask questions that will be answered by church members and pastors in real time.
4. The app will give church members the opportunity to share their learnings, summaries, etc. with others.
5. The app will give church members the opportunity to connect with reading buddies, etc.
6. The app will have a sermon for the day for each day of the devotional.
